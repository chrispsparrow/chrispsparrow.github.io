ABOUT / ME

I'm Christopher Sparrow, an electrical engineer building hardware at the intersection of aerospace systems and embedded electronics. I design the boards, write the firmware, and chase signal integrity all the way down.

At AerospaceNU, Northeastern's high-power rocket team, I developed a custom avionics stack — four-layer PCB, GPS telemetry, LoRa radio uplink — that goes from schematic capture in KiCad straight to flight. The hardware has to work on the first drop.

Dogtooth Systems is my engineering signature — named after the deep-water dogtooth tuna. It's the mark I put on my PCB silkscreens, the aesthetic thread running through my work, and a reminder that high performance exists in environments most people never see.

Currently seeking roles in hardware engineering, avionics, and embedded systems where precision is the baseline expectation, not the exception.
