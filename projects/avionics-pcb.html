ARCHITECTURE

The design centres on a single STM32F4 hub that arbitrates four independent SPI/UART buses. Separating the GPS, radio, IMU, and SD card onto discrete peripherals eliminates bus contention during peak logging rates and gives each subsystem a hard interrupt path to the MCU.

The 4-layer stackup places the inner GND plane immediately beneath the signal layer, minimising return-path inductance under the RF traces. The LoRa antenna feed is routed as a 50 Ω coplanar waveguide strip, length-matched to 80 mm with a keepout enforced in DRC. A π-network matching circuit sits at the SMA breakout for field tuning.

Power enters via a 2-cell LiPo connector, stepped down through a synchronous buck converter to 5 V and then to 3.3 V and 1.8 V LDOs for the GPS module's core and I/O domains. Current sensing resistors on each rail enable power budgeting in firmware.

FIRMWARE

IN PROGRESS

Hardware is validated and populated. Firmware development in progress on STM32CubeIDE. Target: stable GPS fix acquisition, reliable LoRa packet transmission, and verified SD write throughput before first flight.

01
GPS NMEA Parsing
Configure u-blox via UBX protocol, enable GGA + RMC sentences, parse fix and altitude in interrupt callback.
02
LoRa Packet Tx
Initialise SX1276 via SPI, frame a telemetry packet (lat/lon/alt/RSSI), transmit on 10 Hz schedule with CRC.
03
IMU Integration
ICM-20689 DMP bring-up, calibrate gyro offsets, integrate acceleration for apogee detection trigger.
04
SD Write Throughput
FatFS layer over SPI, verify 200 Hz logging without buffer overflow. Pre-allocate clusters to avoid fragmentation delay mid-flight.
05
Flight State Machine
Pad → Boost → Coast → Apogee → Descent states. Pyro continuity check pre-flight, drogue + main ejection commands gated on state.
06
Ground Station
Python receiver decodes LoRa packets, plots live telemetry on map. Target: real-time altitude and GPS track on launch day.
