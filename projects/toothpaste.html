OVERVIEW

A hands-free automatic toothpaste dispenser designed and built as a 3-person capstone project. The device detects a toothbrush via an IR proximity sensor, then drives a servo-powered plunger mechanism to dispense a measured amount of toothpaste — no contact required.

The mechanical assembly was modelled in SolidWorks with full parametric tolerancing. An Arduino Nano manages sensor polling, servo PWM output, and an LED status indicator. The enclosure was 3D-printed in PLA at ±0.2 mm tolerance.

My role covered the complete electronics and firmware side — schematic, wiring harness, sensor calibration, servo characterisation, and all Arduino firmware — while teammates owned mechanical modelling and documentation.
