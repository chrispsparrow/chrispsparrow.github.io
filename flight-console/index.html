Flight Console

The Flight Console plays back telemetry from my rocket avionics boards. It shows each rocket's position on a map, its altitude and speed, and the events of the flight. Every flight in the library says whether it is simulated or real.

My GPS and radio board doesn't sense apogee or the parachutes opening. The console figures those out from the altitude data, and every event says how it was found. If a board reports an event itself, the console shows that too.
