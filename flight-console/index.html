    How to connect

    1. Use Chrome or Edge on a desktop or laptop. Other browsers can't open serial ports.
    2. Close any other program that has the board's port open, like the serial monitor in Arduino or PlatformIO. Only one program can use a port at a time.
    3. Plug the board in, press Connect and pick its port. The console opens it at 115,200 baud.
    4. Nothing is uploaded. The readings stay in this tab until you close it, unless you save them as a CSV file.
